FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

What does the scan actually do?+
It reads your public site the way a buyer would, what you sell, who it is for and how you talk, then matches that to TikTok formats whose comment sections show buying intent. No long questionnaire and no setup.
Is this just ChatGPT with extra steps?+
No. It is grounded in real TikToks and their actual comment sections, then ranks formats by buyer questions rather than a generic guess. Every score links to the posts behind it, so you can check the work.
Why comments instead of views?+
Views tell you people watched. Comments like “link?” and “how much?” tell you people wanted to buy. We rank by the second signal, because that is the one that moves product.
Does it work for apps, not just products?+
Yes. Paste an app site and it finds the formats that convert for consumer apps, not only physical products.
What do I get before I pay?+
A free scan with your ranked formats and example hooks, so you can see the output before you start a trial.
Do I need a creator?+
No. The brief is shot by shot with on screen text and a CTA, so you can film it on your phone or hand it straight to a creator.
Can I cancel the trial?+
Yes, in one click from Billing. The 3 day Pro trial does not charge if you cancel in time.
